SECTION 2 – WHO WE ARE

UMA is a cultural initiative operating under UMA Foundation, an incorporated Ontario non profit organization.

Named in honor of founder Sathish Bala’s mother, UMA represents the strength and resilience of immigrant families whose sacrifice built opportunity for the next generation.

UMA transforms that generational resilience into structured cultural infrastructure. We protect identity, strengthen belonging, and expand access to creative and economic opportunity.

UMA operates year round through programming, mentorship, public events, and strategic partnerships that advance South Asian arts and culture across Ontario.

⸻

SECTION 3 – WHAT WE DO

Cultural Preservation

We deliver multilingual and intergenerational programming that ensures heritage remains active, practiced, and relevant within Canada’s evolving arts landscape.

Year Round Programming

UMA operates continuous programming throughout the year, including workshops, artist development initiatives, mentorship sessions, community gatherings, and public showcases that strengthen participation and creative growth.

Flagship Initiative: DESIFEST

UMA is the driving force behind DESIFEST, Canada’s leading South Asian music festival. For nearly two decades, DESIFEST has provided a major public platform for emerging and established artists while strengthening cultural visibility at a national level.

Creative Economic Pathways

We support artists and creatives through grant readiness guidance, entrepreneurship workshops, production labs, and structured pathways to viable economic opportunity within the creative sector.

Micro Funding and Curator Support

UMA provides micro funding and support to independent curators and community organizers, enabling grassroots cultural initiatives and expanding community led programming capacity across the region.

⸻

SECTION 4 – WHY IT MATTERS

Immigrant communities contribute significantly to Canada’s cultural and economic landscape, yet structured pathways for preservation, participation, and advancement remain limited.

UMA addresses this gap by:

• Protecting and advancing cultural identity
• Increasing equitable access to arts participation
• Supporting youth leadership and creative confidence
• Strengthening social cohesion
• Contributing to Ontario’s creative economy
• Enabling community led cultural initiatives through micro funding

By combining heritage preservation with forward facing opportunity, UMA ensures culture is not only remembered, but advanced.

⸻

SECTION 5 – GOVERNANCE AND STRUCTURE

UMA operates under UMA Foundation, an incorporated Ontario non profit governed by a Board of Directors.

The Foundation maintains transparent financial oversight, regulatory compliance, and community accountability.

UMA builds upon a proven track record of cultural programming and large scale public engagement through initiatives such as DESIFEST and ongoing year round programming.

⸻

SECTION 6 – VISION

UMA is designed as permanent cultural infrastructure.

We anchor identity while moving culture forward. We strengthen belonging while expanding opportunity.

Through disciplined governance, measurable outcomes, year round programming, and strategic partnerships, UMA advances inclusive cultural participation across Ontario.

UMA converts private sacrifice into public structure. It builds for generations.
