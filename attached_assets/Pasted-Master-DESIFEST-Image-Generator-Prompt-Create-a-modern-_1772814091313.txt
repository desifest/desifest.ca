Master DESIFEST Image Generator Prompt

Create a modern, culturally grounded illustration for a South Asian music platform in Toronto.

The scene should feel realistic, urban, and contemporary, reflecting the South Asian diaspora music scene.

Avoid fantasy or traditional costume imagery.

Focus on young artists performing live music in a modern city environment. Include elements like microphones, DJ equipment, guitars, keyboards, or drums. The performers should represent a mix of South Asian identities and modern street style.

The setting should feel like an outdoor music stage in downtown Toronto, with a diverse crowd enjoying the performance.

Style should resemble editorial illustration or poster art, inspired by modern music festival branding.

Color palette should align with DESIFEST branding:

deep purple
electric neon accents
warm stage lighting
modern graphic contrast

The mood should feel:

energetic
inclusive
authentic
community-driven

Avoid:

fantasy temples
exaggerated cultural costumes
overly decorative Bollywood-style scenes
symmetrical AI-looking compositions

The image should feel like a cultural music movement happening now, not a historical or fantasy scene.
