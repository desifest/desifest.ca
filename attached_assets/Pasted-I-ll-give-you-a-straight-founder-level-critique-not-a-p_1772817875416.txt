First, I like the direction. A few things are working well.

What’s working

1. The topic is strong.
“Indie artists and the social media challenge” is a real problem artists face today. That makes it a good evergreen blog topic.

2. It positions DESIFEST as an ecosystem voice.
That’s important. Blogs should not just promote the festival. They should show that DESIFEST understands the artist journey.

3. The message is honest.
The idea that organic reach is collapsing for artists is real. Platforms now only show content to a small slice of followers unless engagement happens quickly.

What needs improvement

1. The article starts a little generic
The opening should feel more personal and scene-aware.

Right now it reads like a general marketing article.

You want it to sound like someone who actually runs a platform where artists struggle with this daily.

For example the opening could feel closer to:

Almost every independent artist today is facing the same quiet frustration. You release music, post consistently, and still feel invisible online. The assumption is that the music is the problem. Most of the time it isn’t. The system changed.

That tone pulls people in faster.

⸻

2. The article needs one DESIFEST insight
Right now the blog talks about social media in general.

Add one moment like this:

At DESIFEST we see this constantly. Artists who have been posting for years with little traction suddenly perform in front of a real crowd and everything changes. The difference is connection.

That anchors the blog in real experience.

⸻

3. The article should end with momentum
Right now the ending is a bit soft.

The blog should land with a clear cultural insight, not just advice.

Something like:

Algorithms change every year. Community does not. The artists who last are the ones who keep showing up, finishing songs, and finding real audiences wherever they can.

That reinforces the DESIFEST philosophy.
