Product Vision

DESIFEST will evolve from a festival website into a year round platform that connects South Asian music creators.

The platform will:

• Convert artist submissions into public profiles
• Enable discovery of artists and collaborators
• Surface music charts and trends
• Provide a central board for gig and collaboration opportunities

This positions DESIFEST as infrastructure for the South Asian music ecosystem, not just an annual event.
